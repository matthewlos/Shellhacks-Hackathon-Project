# TreeHacks winners (2024, 2025, 2026) vs GridLock

Scraped 2026-09-25 from the Devpost galleries (treehacks-2024/2025/2026.devpost.com/project-gallery) and each winner's Devpost page. Prizes are copied exactly as the Devpost project page lists them. GitHub = first GitHub link on the Devpost page.

Counts: 2024 = 60 winner entries (one is named "Test Project", see note), 2025 = 70, 2026 = 64. Total 194.

## Top 5 closest to GridLock

### 1. <PriorityQueue> (2025)

- Prize: Elastic: Best use of Elasticsearch Serverless ($2,000 Cash [1st] & $1,000 Cash [2nd])
- Why close: Puts power plants and projects waiting in the grid interconnection queue on a Mapbox map, groups nearby projects into clusters with Elastic geospatial search, and lets a grid manager approve or reject them. Same world as GridLock (grid transmission planning, FERC Order 2023) and the same core move: group projects by location so they can share one build.
- What to borrow: Their clustering idea (nearby projects share upgrade costs) is basically our coordination-opportunity pitch; copy the Mapbox + geo-query setup and the 'shared cost split' angle for our cost estimate.
- Devpost: https://devpost.com/software/priorityqueue
- GitHub: https://github.com/shrenikpatel17/treeHacks2025

### 2. Tribune (2026)

- Prize: [Anthropic] Human Flourishing Track (1st Place: 4 tungsten cubes 2nd Place: 1 year of Claude Pro 3rd Place: 6 months of Claude Pro)
- Why close: Scrapes city council agendas, municipal codes and government PDFs overnight, pulls out ~200 policies, geo-tags the affected neighborhoods, and shows them on a Mapbox map. That is the hardest part of GridLock: turning messy public government documents into clean, mapped records.
- What to borrow: Their PDF-to-structured-records pipeline (scrape, LLM extract, geo-tag, cite the source clause) is what we need for utility IRP/transmission plan PDFs; also borrow 'every record links back to its source page' so judges trust the data.
- Devpost: https://devpost.com/software/tribune
- GitHub: https://github.com/arihantjain4/Tribune

### 3. GridVeda (2026)

- Prize: [Stanford Ecopreneurship] Sustainability: Best prototyping process
- Why close: Power-grid tool for utility operators: watches 20 substation transformers, gives each a live health score, and alerts operators when failure risk passes 50%. Same buyer (utilities) and a similar output: scored risks that tell the operator what to act on first.
- What to borrow: Their health/risk scoring UI and 'alert above a threshold' pattern map straight onto our touching / 1.6 km / 8 km / 40 km tiers; the utility-industry framing (aging assets, outage cost stats) is good pitch material.
- Devpost: https://devpost.com/software/gridveda
- GitHub: https://github.com/TheRaven5520/GridVeda

### 4. ContainOS (2026)

- Prize: [OpenAI] Artificial Intelligence Track ([1st] Lunch with OpenAI engineers at the office + 1 year of ChatGPT Pro [2nd] 1 year of ChatGPT Pro [3rd] OpenAI swag)
- Why close: Wildfire command dashboard on a Leaflet map that pulls in wind, terrain, infrastructure (including powerlines) and population data, then produces prioritized alerts. Close on interactive map + many public data layers + ranked alerts.
- What to borrow: Their pattern of 'compute the hard numbers deterministically first, then let the AI explain them' fits us: do distance/timeline overlap in plain code (PostGIS/Shapely), use the LLM only to write the why for each flag.
- Devpost: https://devpost.com/software/containos
- GitHub: https://github.com/maanitg/containment

### 5. ERWIN: Enhanced Rock Weathering Impact Navigator (2025)

- Prize: Sustainability Prize: Best solves the user's pain point of a provided challenge ($800 Cash)
- Why close: Carbon-removal tool: pick a region on a Mapbox map, enter project parameters, and it scrapes public soil/weather data and runs a science model to estimate impact. Close on the 'map + public data + impact estimate' piece.
- What to borrow: Borrow the pick-an-area-then-get-an-estimate flow for our cost/impact estimate on each overlap (miles of shared corridor, dollars saved).
- Devpost: https://devpost.com/software/erwin-enhanced-rock-weathering-impact-navigator
- GitHub: https://github.com/Jackal-Studios/rock-thing

Honorable mentions: CalTrack 2026 (wildfire map with population density and risk overlays, [NVIDIA] Edge AI Track), Plot 2025 (location insights on Mapbox, LangChain prize), SkySplat 2024 (drone 3D models for infrastructure inspection, Sustainability Grand Prize).

Honest note: no TreeHacks winner in these three years does exactly GridLock (two utilities' transmission plans + distance/timeline overlap). PriorityQueue is the only one about transmission/interconnection planning.
