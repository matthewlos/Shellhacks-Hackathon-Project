`../farm-hand/laptop/static/soil_shader.js`, which adapts the soil and water shader
from `web/src/scene/shaders.ts` and the pour timing from
`web/src/scene/FieldScene.ts` of https://github.com/SamisLife/PromptGrassGrowGrass.
