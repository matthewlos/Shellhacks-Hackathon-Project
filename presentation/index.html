A timer waters on the clock, not on the soil.

In a drought a grower can't afford to lose the crop, or to waste water keeping it alive. A dead crop also costs the months it took to grow.

Farm Hand pours when the soil reads dry.

A probe reads the soil, a small model makes the call, and the probe checks how much water went in.

  1. Read

    Soil moisture and temperature, one reading a second.

  2. Decide

    Laya makes the call. The Gemini team checks it and says why.

  3. Pour

    The ESP32 runs the pump and stops at the target. Then it reads again.

One working box today. Outdoors, a rain forecast can tell it to wait.
