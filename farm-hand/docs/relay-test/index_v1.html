Relay test: 3 wires, nothing else

USB unplugged while you wire. No breadboard, no pump. Match the pin labels printed on your boards.

THE 3 WIRES · F-M jumpers
1ESP32 VIN → relay DC+VIN = the pin right beside the USB port. It is the 5V power.
2ESP32 GND → relay DC−The GND pin right next to VIN.
3ESP32 D26 → relay INThe on/off signal. It carries no power.

Hole end on the ESP32 pin. Pin end into the screw: loosen, push in, tighten hard, tug.

THEN PLUG IN THE USB. YOU SHOULD SEE
PWR light bright + a click every 2 s
  • Bright + clicking → relay is good. The problem was the breadboard or a loose wire.
  • Faint or dark → tighten DC+ / DC−, check wire 1 is on VIN (not 3V3). Still faint → swap in a new relay from the pack.
