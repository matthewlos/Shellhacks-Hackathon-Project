Home Theater Speakers: Bring Home the Boom

The roar of a game day crowd, the 360 degree battlefield of a first person shooter match, the pristine sound design of your favorite big screen spectacle - a home theater speaker system brings it all into your living room.

Don’t settle for less when it comes to sound quality - we have brought together a selection of the best soundbars and surround sound systems, including tons of options with support for the latest AV technologies like Dolby Atmos. Enjoy killer home audio right out of the box with a soundbar and subwoofer package, or take it to the next level with a full surround sound audio system.

Find the Best Home Theater System for You

Soundbar home theater systems offer awesome audio performance at a variety of price points, along with a huge variety of features. These key factors can affect your decision when you are evaluating home theater sound systems:

Number of Channels: Generally, speaker channels are what give surround sound its spacious and ultra defined feel. The so called 5.1 standard is a baseline for home speaker systems: three front facing channels, two surround channels - which may be on the ends of a soundbar or in separate rear speakers, and one subwoofer channel. Systems built for more channels can offer additional layers of sound experience.

Ports and Connectors: Note which types of audio inputs and outputs a speaker system offers. Some connect via HDMI Arc, while others via traditional RCA connectors, 3.5mm jacks, or even USB. Make sure that other elements of your system offer compatible connectors to minimize the number of cables and adapters that you need to hook it up.

Wireless Connectivity: Many sound systems offer the option to connect wireless speakers and wireless subwoofers via Bluetooth or WiFi. This can be a big advantage when creating a clean setup with easy cable management. If you want a high end look, add bookshelf speakers or floor standing speakers that connect seamlessly. Some models also offer Apple AirPlay connectivity, allowing you to seamlessly play your favorite music, podcasts, and more. Create your wireless home theater system with Micro Center today.

Dolby Atmos DTS Virtual X Support: True multi directional audio is the next great frontier in home theater sound, with upward firing speakers added to the mix to create a truly immersive experience. Dolby Atmos and DTS Virtual X are the two major technologies that offer this, so look for a speaker system that supports either of these if you are looking for the most powerful audio experience available.
