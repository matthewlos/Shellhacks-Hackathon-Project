How GridLock finds where two power companies' plans collide

Step 1

Two neighbors each plan their own construction.

Georgia Power and Dominion Energy South Carolina sit across the Savannah River. Neither sees the other's list.

Step 2

Put both lists on one map.

Substations become dots, new power lines become lines. One color per company.

Step 3

Measure the closest gap between every pair.

Edge to edge, not center to center. A long line can pass right by the other company's substation.

Step 4

Anything under 40 km gets flagged, and closer ranks higher.

40 km is about how far a crew drives from one staging yard in a morning.

Step 5

Same years break the tie.

Close on the map is the main signal. Close on the calendar pushes it up the list.

Step 6

Hand in a clickable map and a ranked list.

Bonus points for a rough dollars or land-saved estimate on one of them. Most pairs won't overlap, and that's expected.
