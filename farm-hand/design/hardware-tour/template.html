The real build, shown on its 3D model. Tap a dot or a name to see what the part does, where it plugs in, and what it did in testing on Sep 23.

Not in the 3D model

Transistor + 1K resistor

A PN2222 on the breadboard. It lets the ESP32's weak 3.3 V pin click the 5 V relay. Without it the relay stuck on and never clicked.

Not in the 3D model

Temp probe adapter

Small board the temp probe screws into. It has the pull-up resistor built in, so no extra 4.7k is needed. The 3D model still shows that old 4.7k resistor.

Whole build

$72.94 in parts

ESP32 3-pack, 5 moisture probes, 5 temp probes, 4 pumps, 4 relays and a starter kit, all from Amazon. Most of it is spares.

Numbers are from the Sep 23 bring-up on the real board (PLAN.md sections 2 and 3). The pump's 20 ml a second is an estimate until it's measured with a cup.
